# Crochet Mini Santa-Hat Keychain

A 15-round worked hat/brim disc in red with a white glitter brim, folded and finished with a pompom on a keychain clip.

## Materials
- Yarn: 5-ply, 50% cotton / 50% acrylic, medium thickness
- Hooks: 2.5 mm
- Colours: red, white (or silver glitter)
- Notions: keychain clip, small pompom, glue, scissors

## Abbreviations
- ch = Chain
- sc = Single crochet
- sl st = Slip stitch
- magic ring = Ring of adjustable loops worked over first (MR)
- x = 1 single crochet
- v = 2 single crochets in the top of the next stitch (increase)

## Gauge & finished dimensions
- Gauge: 30.0 sts x 36.0 rows / 10 cm (ASSUMED, not measured from a swatch)
- Widest diameter: ~2.12 cm [1.91-2.33 cm] (from the widest round's circumference)
- Worked height: ~4.17 cm [3.75-4.59 cm] (rows x row gauge -- how tall the piece is as worked, not a 3D measurement)
- NOTE: ASSUMED gauge. You gave yarn and hook but no swatch, and no swatch means no measured size. 30 sts x 36 rows / 10 cm is a typical tight-tension amigurumi gauge on 2.5 mm with 5-ply, so treat every centimetre below as illustrative. Work a 10 x 10 cm swatch, count it, and re-run to make these exact.

## Yarn usage (estimate)

- Covers the stitch-checked parts only, at the quantities listed.
