# Crochet Star Hanging Ornament

## Pattern: finishing operations

### Sewing
  > This seam is worked entirely with yarn and a needle. No glue is used anywhere in it; glue appears in the materials only for the surface decoration.
  - Place the two stars together with wrong sides facing out, lining up the flat bases and all 5 points tip to tip.
  - Thread the yarn needle with about 70 cm of the same yarn.
  - Bring the needle up between the layers at the base of one point so the starting knot is hidden inside.
  - Sew around with an invisible slip-stitch seam: pick up one front loop on the star in front and one back loop on the star behind, pull the yarn through, and repeat one seam stitch per crochet stitch -- 30 seam stitches across the base.
  - At each of the 5 tips take two stitches into the same space so the point turns sharp instead of rounding off.
  - Stop with about 4 cm of the last point left open. Fasten off temporarily.
  - Push small pieces of fiber filling through the opening, working them into each point with the back of a hook so the tips fill without bulging the seam.
  - Close the opening with the same invisible seam, matching stitch for stitch. Pull the yarn tight, pass the needle through the star and out the other side, and cut so the tail disappears inside.
