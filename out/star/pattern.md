# Crochet Star Hanging Ornament

## Pattern: finishing operations

### Sewing
  > This seam is worked entirely with yarn and a needle. No glue is used anywhere in it; glue appears in the materials only for the surface decoration.
  - Join two stars together and sew them together, stopping near the end.
  - Add filling and then sew completely.
  - Place the two stars with their right sides facing out and their wrong sides together inside, lining up the flat bases and all 5 points tip to tip. [ADDED]
      why: The source doesn't say how the two faces are stacked. This orientation is the one the seam below needs; the opposite pairing would leave the ornament inside-out.
  - Thread the yarn needle with about 70 cm of the same yarn. [ADDED]
      why: A working length, not a figure from your source.
  - Bring the needle up between the layers at the base of one point so the starting knot is hidden inside. [ADDED]
  - Sew around with an invisible slip-stitch seam: pick up one front loop on the star in front and one back loop on the star behind, pull the yarn through, and repeat one seam stitch per crochet stitch. [ADDED]
  - At each of the 5 tips take two stitches into the same space so the point turns sharp instead of rounding off. [ADDED]
  - Stop with about 4 cm of the last point left open. Fasten off temporarily. [ADDED]
      why: This is your 'stopping near the end', made measurable.
  - Push small pieces of fiber filling through the opening, working them into each point with the back of a hook so the tips fill without bulging the seam. [ADDED]
      why: Expands your 'Add filling'.
  - Close the opening with the same invisible seam, matching stitch for stitch. Pull the yarn tight, pass the needle through the star and out the other side, and cut so the tail disappears inside. [ADDED]
      why: Expands your 'then sew completely'.

### Hanging
  - Make a sl knot and ch 20, then cut the yarn.
  - Insert it onto the top of the star to make a hanging.
  - Sew the loop into the top seam with two stitches on the inside and bury the tail there. [ADDED]
